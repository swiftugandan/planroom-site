Home design studio for the browser

Draw it to the millimetre. Walk through it in 3D.

Planroom is a floor-plan editor that runs entirely in your browser. Draw rooms and walls with exact dimensions, place doors, windows and furniture, build floors, stairs, roofs and chimneys, look at the same design in 3D, and print its elevations and sections to scale. No account, no server, and it keeps working offline.

one document · every view

One plan, drawn once, seen every way

Every Planroom plan is a single geometric document stored in millimetres. The 2D plan, the 3D view and every export are drawn from it, so a wall that is 4,000 mm in the plan is 4,000 mm in 3D, in the elevation and in the PDF.

Plan

  • Rooms, straight and arc walls, with shared walls and T and X junctions joined automatically
  • Typed lengths and angles, a live read-out and temporary face-to-face dimensions
  • Ortho, Polar, snaps, alignment and construction guides, rulers
  • Trace over a photo or scan after calibrating its scale with two points
  • Move, turn and resize furniture with handles and live clear distances
  • Trace over the storey below, shown as a faint underlay
  • A model check that lists what looks wrong, with a fix for each finding

Doors and windows

  • Openings stay put when walls move, reverse or split
  • Flush, panelled, glazed, French, sliding and pocket doors, and cased openings
  • Sash, fixed, casement, sliding, picture, Georgian and transom windows
  • Segmental, semicircular and pointed heads, fanlights, sidelights and glazing bars
  • Lintels, hoods, voussoirs and stone sills; recesses for fireplaces and alcoves

Furnish and finish

  • 72 original furniture items and fittings, from sofas to showers, radiators and lights, with a period pack
  • Furniture lands against the wall facing the room; fireplaces fit their recess
  • Groups, align and distribute, arrays, mirror and match properties
  • Brick bonds, stone, slate, shingle, siding, herringbone, tile and wallpaper patterns at real scale
  • A materials manager, editable copies and your own image textures

Structure

  • Flat, shed, gable and hip roofs, with pitch, overhang and eave height set per edge
  • Cross-gables and joined roofs with solved valleys; walls attached to the roof
  • Chimney stacks and gable, hipped and shed dormers that cut the roof
  • Slabs, stairwells with guard rails, terraces, columns and beams
  • Railings, fences and gates; a site ground level and walls that start below the floor
  • Straight, L, U and spiral stairs worked out from the floor-to-floor height

Whole home

  • Up to 8 floors, each with up to 8 design variants to compare
  • Dollhouse and eye-level 3D, saved camera views and lighting presets
  • A ground grid in metres under the building, for scale and orientation
  • Show every storey, or ghost the others, without the camera moving
  • Plan and 3D side by side, with one selection, editable in both
  • Command palette, context menus and full keyboard operation

Drawings

  • Orthographic elevations from the view cube
  • Sections along any line, with cut solids filled and the section mark on the plan
  • Cut walls hatched by their core material
  • A plan cut height per floor, with overhead openings dashed
  • Notes sized in millimetres that wrap and print at their true size

Export

  • SVG, PNG and scaled vector PDF with one page per floor
  • Elevation and section PDFs at 1:50 or 1:100
  • 1920×1080 PNG renders of the 3D view
  • Portable .planroom.json project files with images embedded

New in this release: period openings, roofs that join, and drawings you can print

Released 14 October 2026
commit 098a882

Openings as they are built

Doors and windows take segmental, semicircular and pointed heads on straight and curved walls. A door carries a fanlight and sidelights in one frame, a new sash window has its meeting rail and horns, and lintels, voussoirs and stone sills are drawn on the outside face. Recesses make fireplace openings and alcoves, and a window can sit above a door.

Roofs that join

Set pitch, overhang and eave height for each edge, or end an edge against a wall. Type a ridge height and the pitch is solved. Add a cross-gable from an eave and its valleys are solved; close gable ends without a wall, and attach wall tops to the roof above.

Chimneys and dormers

The Chimney tool builds stacks with a cap and pots that cut every roof they pass through. The Dormer tool places gable, hipped or shed dormers on a roof facet, with a window from the opening designs.

Period fittings and patterned finishes

Chimneypieces and grates fit their recess in one click, and 16 period items join the catalog. Brick bonds, stone, slate, shingle, siding, herringbone, tile and wallpaper are drawn to scale, the same in the plan, in 3D and in exports.

Elevations and sections

The view cube opens the four elevations as true orthographic views. Draw a section line and the section view cuts the building and fills the cut. Both export as PDFs at 1:50 or 1:100 with a scale bar and ground line, and cut walls are hatched by their core material.

Also recent (13 October)

Furniture lands against a wall facing the room, and a model check lists what looks wrong with a fix for each finding. A materials manager, a site ground level, walls that start below the floor, railings, fences and gates, the storey below as an underlay, and import by dropping a file.

Your plans stay on your computer

Planroom has no accounts and no backend. Plans are saved in your browser as you work.

Saved locally

"Saved" appears only after the save has finished. If saving fails, your edits stay on screen and you can export them.

Works offline

After the first visit Planroom loads and runs without a connection, and updates in place when you are back online.

Safe recovery

Snapshots let a damaged save be recovered, and a second tab can't overwrite a plan that is open in another.

Yours to keep

Export a .planroom.json file at any time. It opens in any Planroom, with its images inside.

Where it runs

Planroom is built for desktop browsers. Below 900 px wide it opens as a read-only viewer.

Run it yourself

Planroom is a static web app: plain JavaScript modules, Canvas2D for the plan and Three.js for 3D. Build it and serve the dist folder from any web server, or run it locally with Node 22.

Then choose Try demo home sample to open the furnished two-storey house shown on this page.

npm ci
npm run build
npm run preview   # http://127.0.0.1:4175

Each release is also published as a ready-built archive of the app. Storage is per web address, so export a backup before moving between servers.

Planroom is a design tool, not an engineering one. Stairs, slabs, columns and beams make no claim about load capacity or building-code compliance; stair headroom and comfort checks are advisory.
