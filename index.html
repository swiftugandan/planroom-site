Home design studio for the browser

Draw it to the millimetre. Walk through it in 3D.

Planroom is a floor-plan editor that runs entirely in your browser. Draw rooms and walls with exact dimensions, place doors, windows and furniture, build floors, stairs and roofs, and look at the same design in 3D. No account, no server, and it keeps working offline.

one document · every view

One plan, drawn once, seen every way

Every Planroom plan is a single geometric document stored in millimetres. The 2D plan, the 3D view and every export are drawn from it, so a wall that is 4,000 mm in the plan is 4,000 mm in 3D and in the PDF.

Plan

  • Rooms, straight and arc walls, with shared walls and T and X junctions joined automatically
  • Typed lengths and angles, a live read-out and temporary face-to-face dimensions
  • Ortho, Polar, snaps, alignment and construction guides, rulers
  • Trace over a photo or scan after calibrating its scale with two points
  • Move, turn and resize furniture with handles and live clear distances

Doors and windows

  • Openings stay put when walls move, reverse or split
  • Flush, panelled, glazed, French, sliding and pocket doors, and cased openings
  • Fixed, casement, sliding, picture, Georgian and transom windows

Furnish and finish

  • 56 original furniture items and fittings, from sofas to showers, radiators and lights
  • Groups, align and distribute, arrays, mirror and match properties
  • Material presets, editable copies and your own image textures at real scale

Structure

  • Flat, shed, gable and hip roofs, including over L, T and U shapes
  • Slabs, stairwells with guard rails, terraces, columns and beams
  • Straight, L, U and spiral stairs worked out from the floor-to-floor height

Whole home

  • Up to 8 floors, each with up to 8 design variants to compare
  • Dollhouse and eye-level 3D, saved camera views and lighting presets
  • A ground grid in metres under the building, for scale and orientation
  • Show every storey, or ghost the others, without the camera moving
  • Plan and 3D side by side, with one selection, editable in both
  • Command palette, context menus and full keyboard operation

Export

  • SVG, PNG and scaled vector PDF with one page per floor
  • 1920×1080 PNG renders of the 3D view
  • Portable .planroom.json project files with images embedded

New in this release: a ground grid in 3D

Released 12 October 2026
commit 665f0e9

A ground grid in 3D

The building now stands on a ground grid of 1 m squares, with every fifth metre a little stronger. It sits at the plan's ground level, so a basement goes below it, and it reaches 5 m beyond everything built. It takes a quiet tint of the background you choose, never appears in exported pictures, and turns off with Ground grid in the 3D view panel.

Also recent (11 October)

A professional workspace, editing in 3D, split view and storeys in 3D, below.

A one-row workspace

Project, floor, design, scene and saved views now share one app bar with Undo, 2D/3D and Export. Press [ and ] to hide the side panels and give the drawing the width. Zoom and a true scale bar float over the drawing, and the interface has light and dark themes.

Edit furniture where it stands

A selected item shows rotate and resize handles that work in 10 mm steps and 15° turns, with its clear distances to the walls while you drag. A quick actions bar floats by any selection with its most-used commands.

Edit in 3D

Click anything in the dollhouse to select it, drag furniture across its floor with the plan's snaps, fly to a room, and turn the camera with the view cube.

Plan and 3D, side by side

Split view shows the plan and the 3D view together, with one selection. The 3D camera is drawn on the plan as a cone you can drag, and entering 3D swings the camera down out of the plan you were drawing.

The whole building, or just one storey

The Storeys card shows all storeys, those up to the one you are editing, or that one only. Other storeys can be ghosted as translucent massing that clicks pass through. The camera stays where you put it, and PageUp and PageDown move between storeys.

Your plans stay on your computer

Planroom has no accounts and no backend. Plans are saved in your browser as you work.

Saved locally

"Saved" appears only after the save has finished. If saving fails, your edits stay on screen and you can export them.

Works offline

After the first visit Planroom loads and runs without a connection, and updates in place when you are back online.

Safe recovery

Snapshots let a damaged save be recovered, and a second tab can't overwrite a plan that is open in another.

Yours to keep

Export a .planroom.json file at any time. It opens in any Planroom, with its images inside.

Where it runs

Planroom is built for desktop browsers. Below 900 px wide it opens as a read-only viewer.

Run it yourself

Planroom is a static web app: plain JavaScript modules, Canvas2D for the plan and Three.js for 3D. Build it and serve the dist folder from any web server, or run it locally with Node 22.

Then choose Try demo home sample to open the furnished two-storey house shown on this page.

npm ci
npm run build
npm run preview   # http://127.0.0.1:4175

Each release is also published as a ready-built archive of the app. Storage is per web address, so export a backup before moving between servers.

Planroom is a design tool, not an engineering one. Stairs, slabs, columns and beams make no claim about load capacity or building-code compliance; stair headroom and comfort checks are advisory.
